Community edits, report dismissal, and layout fixes.

• Dismiss a reported issue from its pin with a public reason. Dismissed reports leave the map but stay in history.
• The Community edits page now lists every contribution, edit, rollback, report, and dismissal with its type, a shape preview, and Eastern time. Tap an entry to fly to it on the map.
• Community route lines no longer draw a red no-entry fill behind them.
• Web: the Report button and map controls sit at the right edge instead of the center.

Please test dismissing a report, rolling back a contribution, and the Community edits list. Community submissions publish immediately and are not endorsed by Bike Walk Greenville. Check actual access signs, crossings, and conditions; follow traffic laws and use appropriate protective equipment.
